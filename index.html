Ingredient & Sourcing Story
“We believe culinary honesty begins at dawn — selecting local Marathwada spices, churning fresh buttermilk, and honoring pure vegetarian heritage.”

Situated at the transit gateway of Ambajogai, directly opposite the Central ST Bus Depot on Parli Road, Hotel Raghav Veg has welcomed devotees visiting Parli Vaijnath Jyotirlinga, Yogeshwari Devi Temple, and local families for authentic, uncompromised meals.

Our kitchen celebrates two distinct culinary traditions: the fiery, tarri-laced soul of Maharashtrian cuisine with our signature Shev Bhaji, and the luxurious, melt-in-mouth textures of royal Mughlai-Punjabi cooking with our Royal Malai Kofta. We prepare every dish with zero artificial food coloring, pure cow ghee tempering, and completely separate, authentic Jain culinary options.
